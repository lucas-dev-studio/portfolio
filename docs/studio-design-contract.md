# Lucas Studio Design Contract

**Product lens:** A prospective client evaluating Lucas needs to see evidence that he can build a site, automate a process, or integrate AI, then start a WhatsApp conversation. The experience itself demonstrates interactive engineering; the two real projects establish credibility. Assume visitors include small-business owners on phones with no 3D-navigation experience. The spectacle must preserve readable project evidence and an immediate contact route.

**User + job:** Evaluate Lucas's practical capabilities and discuss a project.
**First-read object:** A distinctive fabricated sculpture, supported by Lucas's identity and his three capabilities in at most two short lines.
**Primary action:** Open a conversation with Lucas through WhatsApp.
**Density decision:** Sparse text, large spatial composition. Interpret the requested 85% visual / 15% text as visual hierarchy, never as permission to omit navigation, proof, or labels.
**Hierarchy:** Sculpture; compact identity/offer; chapter cue; persistent contact. In project chapters, the actual work becomes the first-read object.
**Interaction model:** Five authored chapters: sculpture, SANDBOX, educational system, modular services, contact. Scrolling changes composition and spatial relationships. Sites, automation, and AI each produce an observable change in the service scene.
**Materials:** Charcoal #161715, porcelain #e9e6df, satin metal, restrained orange #ff5b24. Material differences must remain visible through lights, bevels, thickness, occlusion, and controlled highlights.
**Responsive priority:** Recompose the sculpture for a portrait camera; preserve its characteristic silhouette. Move supporting text outside the object's focal region. Keep contact and project identity visible without horizontal scrolling.

## Reference patterns and transferable lessons

- [Lusion: Porsche Dream Machine](https://lusion.co/projects/porsche_dream_machine/) describes a four-phase journey through an object's evolution. Adapt the principle of staged transformation: each portfolio chapter needs its own visual state and reason for movement. Do not copy Porsche forms or its film aesthetic.
- [Unseen Studio](https://unseen.co/) exposes explicit drag guidance and an entry choice without audio. Adapt discoverable interaction and optional sensory effects: visible scroll guidance, pause control, and useful static presentation.
- [Lusion: Digital City](https://exp-digital-city.lusion.co/) combines selectable cities with landmark information. Adapt scene controls that select an understandable domain object: service selection changes the visible assembly, and project controls expose actual evidence.
- [Active Theory: The Field](https://medium.com/active-theory/the-field-bbe924426d7f) documents evolving a linear journey into branching stories for user freedom. Adapt direct chapter navigation so a client can reach projects or contact without completing the entire presentation.

These are primary-source interaction and storytelling references, not a visual audit of their WebGL renderings. Active Theory's homepage returned only a JavaScript requirement; no visual conclusions were inferred from it.

## Forbidden defaults

- The previous blue/lime ring or sphere composition.
- Floating primitive collections with no purposeful assembly, material logic, or silhouette.
- Equal service cards, pill galleries, generic metric dashboards, giant promotional paragraphs, gradient/glass ornament.
- Reusing the same object/camera pose in every chapter with only text changes.
- Fictional client logos, fabricated results, invented screenshots, or educational representations presented as actual captures.
- Hover-only discoveries, mandatory sound, or an animation that blocks access to project/contact links.
